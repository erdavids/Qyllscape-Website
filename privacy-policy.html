What is Seven Wishes?

Seven Wishes LLC, also referred to as Seven Wishes, is the company created by Eric Davidson to develop software applications like Qyllscape.

Seven Wishes operates out of North Carolina at 4030 Wake Forest Rd Ste 349, Raleigh NC, 27609.

What data does Seven Wishes collect about me?

Seven Wishes does not sell personal data.

The contact form on the Qyllscape website gives Seven Wishes LLC the opportunity to provide support and receive feedback from users. This form allows users to communicate their name, email address, and message.

This website is served to users by Netlify, and the contact form submissions are collected through Netlify forms. The data is stored on Netlify servers.

Seven Wishes LLC will only ever access the contact form data to internally process feedback and provide support on unique issues. The data will be routinely deleted within 90 days even without direct requests.

In regards to purchasing / billing data, see the section below: Purchasing / Billing Data

Does Seven Wishes store my writing or have access to it?

Seven Wishes LLC does not store your writing, or have access to any project content you have written. What you have written belongs to you.

Writing statistics, analytics, and achievements found within Qyllscape are calculated and stored locally on users' devices.

If you are using the application through Steam, there are Steam specific achievement features that Qyllscape will trigger based on the length of a project, but they do not indicate direct access to your writing. They still represent an internal process in the application that evaluates total word count.

How can I contact Seven Wishes about my data?

You can use the same contact form on the site to directly request that your data is deleted.

If you want your data removed from any of the third-parties mentioned in this policy, you will need to contact them directly.

Purchasing / Billing Data

Qyllscape is available for purchase on more than one platform. Depending on which you choose, relevant data will also be stored on their servers in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Itch.io (https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy)
  • Steam (https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/)

These platforms act as third-party vendors (Merchants of Record) for purchasing Qyllscape. Seven Wishes LLC has access to certain purchasing metrics that are exposed through these third-party vendors, but Seven Wishes LLC does not store your card numbers or bank information.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

Seven Wishes LLC may update this privacy policy occasionally. Any changes that represent substantial privacy changes will be communicated by updates to this document.
